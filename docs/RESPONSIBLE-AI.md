# Responsible AI

HomeFlow AI applies a human-in-the-loop model.

## AI may
- prioritise projects for review
- explain data-driven project-health factors
- identify unusual budget/progress divergence
- summarise evidence and activity
- draft recovery actions
- generate executive briefings

## AI must not autonomously
- approve inspections
- certify physical completion
- approve expenditure
- sanction or blacklist contractors
- make procurement awards
- determine beneficiary eligibility
- make legal findings

Every AI output should distinguish observed facts, calculated indicators, interpretation and recommended action. If evidence is insufficient, the system must state that explicitly.
